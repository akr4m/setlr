## Views within existing pages

- Transaction details open by selecting an activity row on the dashboard or Transaction history page.
- Payment review, authorization, processing, results, and receipts appear within the corresponding payment page.
- Wallet balance details, financial-product forms, card controls, and legal sections appear within their listed pages or dialogs.
